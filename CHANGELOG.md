# Changelog

## Unreleased

- The in-game seat menu on the monitor too (#c137c681, launcher side; needs Lens's renderer side): turn on **Controls → Seat → Seat menu on the monitor too (Launch)**, and the seat menu key or button opens the same menu over the game when you play with **Launch**. There **PgUp** / **RB** and **PgDn** / **LB** also make the cockpit view wider or narrower. Launch installs DiRT3VR's renderer for those sessions only and removes it after exit.
- **Anisotropic filtering** under **Graphics → Desktop** and **Graphics → VR rendering** (#15714dd2, as in DiRT2VR; needs Wrench's renderer side): sharper road and ground textures at an angle, from Off to 16×. DiRT 3 has no such option, so DiRT3VR's renderer applies it. On the monitor, a choice other than **Use game setting** installs the renderer for that Launch session and removes it after DiRT 3 closes.
- **Wheel rotation…** in **Controls → Driving controls** (#a72b44ea, as in DiRT2VR): enter your wheel's rotation and the rotation you want in DiRT 3, for example 540° on a 900° wheel, and it sets the steering saturation so you reach full lock there. Wheels with a fixed range, such as the G920, get their maximum suggested.
- A clutch pedal bound under **Controls → Driving controls** now starts at a 10% dead zone and full clutch at 80% travel, as in DiRT 3's own wheel presets, for **Manual: H-pattern with clutch** (#16fa2aa3). A clutch still at the earlier 0% / 100% moves to it when the controls load.
- Direct practice now drives with your wheel or controller (#16fa2aa3). A direct start skips DiRT 3's Press Start screen, so it used to pick the keyboard and ignore a wheel such as the G920. It now uses the device you bound steering to under **Controls → Driving controls**.
- The **Live input and force feedback** window now opens tall enough to show your controller or wheel down to **Test rumble** / **Test force feedback**, also with Windows display scaling above 100% (#2a6e6f28). With several devices it fills the screen and scrolls.
- Launch VR no longer refuses a VR render scale that is too high for your headset (#7f93667d). DiRT3VR can render at most 4096 pixels per side, so with a Quest 3 at Virtual Desktop's 3072 × 3264 the most is 125%. A higher scale now runs at that maximum, and the launch message and **Graphics → VR rendering** say so.
- **Recommend for this PC** (#68e16dc9) under **Graphics → Quality**: after a VR session, it suggests a VR render scale, or the VR Balanced or Potato preset, from that session's measured frame rate. It only changes the controls; Undo preset brings them back.
- FPS in VR (#4896ffd3): **Graphics → VR HUD → Show FPS and GPU usage in the headset** adds a small performance display, and the last VR session's measured frame rate (median, low, GPU usage and what held it back) shows under **Graphics → VR rendering**.
- Toggle VR (#288ceec1): press **F6** during Launch VR to switch the headset between VR and a flat screen in front of you, for a view that looks wrong or uncomfortable; press it again to go back. Change the key, or add a controller or wheel button, under **Controls → VR and seat controls**.
- Place the VR HUD (#3652b9aa): **Graphics → VR HUD** sets how far away the HUD and menu panel is (1 to 5 m; it keeps the same size in your view) and whether it follows your view or stays where you recentered.
- Cockpit field of view on the monitor (#b330cc4f): **Controls → Seat → Cockpit field of view (monitor)** makes the cockpit view up to 30° wider or 20° narrower than DiRT 3's own when you play with **Launch**. VR always uses your headset's field of view.
- A steadier cockpit in VR (#cd56379b): DiRT 3 no longer moves and shakes the cockpit camera by itself under braking, cornering and over bumps, since your head already moves the view. You can turn this off, and hide the driver or the co-driver, under **Controls → Seat**.
- An in-game seat menu for VR (#bd820b32): during Launch VR, press **F11** to move your seat in the headset with the arrow keys (hold Shift to move sideways), then Enter to save or Esc to cancel. An Xbox controller's D-pad, A, B and X work too, and a wheel's hat moves the seat. Under **Controls → Seat** you can change the key or add a controller or wheel button that opens the menu (#c137c681).
- In Controls → Driving controls, a row now lights up in the launcher's accent colour while its button, d-pad direction, axis or key is held, so you can see what each input does (#9ff647cf).
- SteamVR's desktop theater no longer opens over the game during SteamVR sessions (#24867b69, as in GRID2VR). The launcher turns that SteamVR setting off for the session and puts your own setting back afterwards; Restore original files does it too if a session ended unexpectedly. SteamVR must already be running for this.
- SteamVR works with Launch VR again (#da2bcde1). It was switched off after one upside-down report from a session with another VR game open; it is new and still being checked, so please report any upside-down, tilted or mirrored picture with the session logs.
